LOVE IS AN EPIC JOURNEY

Taylor and Ben first crossed paths at Eastern Washington University, where Taylor was an assistant volleyball coach and Ben was an assistant basketball coach. Their shared passion for sports and coaching quickly turned into a friendship, and before long, something more. From post practice chats to cheering each other on during game days, their bond grew stronger with every season. What started as two coaches chasing their dreams turned into a love story they never saw coming and now, they’re so excited to celebrate the next chapter with you!
